# ATLAS v1 open problem

## Frozen question

For the Li--Li--Liao unequal-mass non-hierarchical catalog of word `bABabaBAba`, decide the continuation-connected family decomposition, and on each component compute the planar linear-Floquet critical graph in the `(m1,m2)` plane at `m3=1`: mechanism-labeled edges, organizers, folds, and independently verified qualitative events.

This is not a solution of the general three-body problem. It is a finite numerical statement about one frozen catalog.

## What is already earned (2026-08-15)

These statements are allowed in the manuscript because they have independent continuation or BigFloat/physical records. They do **not** close the open problem by themselves.

1. **One family.** Inside the declared catalog domain the 135,445 rows form one continuation-connected component. The 2023 two-set reading of the scale-invariant `(T,L)` plot is a folded non-injective projection, not a dynamical split. Gate C passed.
2. **Principal lower `+1`.** Independently bound physical plus-one U→S crossing near `(m1,m2)=(0.8023446114, 0.7540187220)` with `m2` bracket width `1.58e-9`.
3. **Principal upper collision.** Independently bound opposite-Krein Hamiltonian--Hopf event near `(m1,m2)=(0.8022889781, 0.7592347066)` with `m2` bracket width `6.27e-9`.
4. **Three mixed organizers.** Independently bound preimages of `(alpha,beta)=(4,4)`:
   - principal left `(0.9292391921, 0.8853664625)`
   - secondary left `(0.9967681995, 0.9560193602)`
   - principal right `(1.0495531867, 1.1294758073)`
5. **Universal spectral algebra.** The reduced trace polynomial and its three walls (`P(+2)=0`, `P(-2)=0`, `Delta=0`) plus the exact vertices `(0,-4)`, `(4,4)`, `(8,28)` are structural mathematics, not a catalog discovery.

- A denser S/U scatter plot.
- ML proposals without Newton correction.
- Float64-only labels at a multiplier collision.
- Linear Floquet language sold as KAM or spatial stability.
- Calling the project solved because CI is green.
