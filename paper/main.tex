\begin{abstract}
The Li--Li--Liao 2021 catalog reports $135{,}445$ unequal-mass non-hierarchical
periodic orbits and $13{,}315$ linearly stable samples on a $0.001$ mass grid.
A 2023 analysis of the same table argued that the set is two families.
We treat family identity as continuation connectivity, not as a plot of
scale-invariant invariants, and we treat the linear-stability boundary as the
preimage of a universal Floquet domain rather than as a rectangular S/U mask.

\textbf{Earned results.}
(i)~The frozen catalog is one continuation-connected component in the declared
gauge.
(ii)~The principal lower transition at $m_1\approx0.80234$ is an independently
reproduced physical $+1$ crossing with $m_2$ uncertainty $1.58\times10^{-9}$.
(iii)~The principal upper transition at $m_1\approx0.80229$ is an independently
reproduced opposite-Krein Hamiltonian--Hopf event with $m_2$ uncertainty
$6.27\times10^{-9}$.
(iv)~The three coarse $+1/-1$ switches are independently reproduced mixed
organizers at $(\alpha,\beta)=(4,4)$.

\textbf{Not earned.}
The connected critical graph is unfinished: $0/620$ catalog cells are frozen as
graph edges; the secondary-lobe birth and death are unclassified; the lower
$+1$ daughter is unclassified.
Scientific status remains OPEN.
This is not a solution of the general three-body problem, and ``stable'' here
means planar linear Floquet stability only.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem, and what it is not}

The general Newtonian three-body problem is not being solved.
The v1 question is finite \cite{li2021stable,stancevic2023moth,li2025review}:

\begin{quote}
Is the Li--Li--Liao unequal-mass non-hierarchical catalog one
continuation-connected family or several?
On each component, what is the planar linear-Floquet critical graph in the
$(m_1,m_2)$ plane at $m_3=1$?
\end{quote}

Li, Li and Liao continued one family on $m_3=1$, $m_1\in[0.8,1.1]$,
$m_2\in[0.7,1.2]$, spacing $0.001$, and plotted a stable domain
\cite{li2021stable}.
Those $135{,}445$ orbits and $13{,}315$ stable labels are literature facts.
ATLAS may not claim them.

Stan\v{c}evi\'c, Vasiljevi\'c and Dmitra\v{s}inovi\'c argued that the same
table is two independent sets, using mass-corrected period and angular-momentum
relations \cite{stancevic2023moth}.
That is the sharpest competing reading of the exact catalog.
We accept their diagnostic as a plot, and reject it as a definition of a
dynamical family.

A later review confirms that unequal-mass continuation, topology, and
high-precision linear stability remain active \cite{li2025review}.
Equal-mass stable-orbit searches \cite{hristov2025stable} and restricted-problem
continuation papers are methodological neighbors, not this catalog.

\section{Equations and the universal Floquet domain}

Planar Newtonian motion with $G=1$ is
\begin{equation}
\ddot{\mathbf r}_i=\sum_{j\ne i}m_j\frac{\mathbf r_j-\mathbf r_i}{\lVert\mathbf r_j-\mathbf r_i\rVert^3}.
\end{equation}
Screening uses DOP853 in IEEE binary64.
Publication truth uses an independent Julia BigFloat Vern9 stack and the
physical symplectic quotient $E^\omega/E$ in canonical Jacobi coordinates,
$E=\mathrm{span}\{X_H,X_L\}$, not deletion of four raw multipliers.

After center-of-mass reduction the monodromy has four trivial/symmetry unit
multipliers.
The remaining four form two reciprocal pairs.
With $t=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}$,
\[
P(t)=t^2-(\alpha-4)t+\beta-4\alpha+8,
\]
where $\alpha=\mathrm{tr}\,M$ and
$\beta=((\mathrm{tr}\,M)^2-\mathrm{tr}(M^2))/2$.
Spectral stability of both pairs is equivalent to both roots of $P$ lying in
$[-2,2]$.
The three walls are
\begin{align}
P(+2)&=\beta-6\alpha+20=0,\\
P(-2)&=\beta-2\alpha+4=0,\\
\Delta&=\alpha^2+8\alpha-16-4\beta=0.
\end{align}
The exact spectral vertices are
$(0,-4)$ (double $-1$), $(4,4)$ (mixed $+1/-1$), and $(8,28)$ (double $+1$).
A smooth $+1/-1$ switch on one continuation sheet must pass through $(4,4)$
unless a coarse cell aliases several roots.
The mass-space critical set is the preimage of this universal boundary under
the continuation-sheet map $(m_1,m_2)\mapsto(\alpha,\beta)$.

\section{The event gate has almost no margin, and less resolution than margin}
\label{sec:margin}

The localized census is gated at $|G|\le 2\times10^{-8}$ and $\|\text{closure}\|
\le 10^{-7}$.  Both gates are frozen and are never loosened anywhere in this
work.  What follows is a statement about how much that first gate is worth.

Across the $620$ localized roots the event residual has median
$3.29\times10^{-9}$, ninetieth percentile $1.58\times10^{-8}$, ninety-ninth
percentile $1.90\times10^{-8}$, and maximum $1.9897980818583960\times10^{-8}$.
The census therefore passes its own gate by $0.511\%$: the worst cell occupies
$99.489\%$ of the budget.  $165$ of the $620$ roots exceed $10^{-8}$ and seven
exceed $95\%$ of the gate.  Any sentence reporting that the census ``passes the
$2\times10^{-8}$ gate'' is incomplete unless it also reports that margin.

The margin is not the binding problem.  The binding problem is that
$2\times10^{-8}$ is finer than the accuracy with which the event functional can
be evaluated at these orbits.  Holding the recorded state fixed and recomputing
$G$ with the locked dependency versions, moving only the integrator tolerance
from $\mathrm{rtol}=5\times10^{-13}$ to $5\times10^{-14}$, moves $G$ by a median
$1.48\times10^{-7}$, which is $7.4$ times the gate; $20$ of $24$ probed cells --
the twelve worst offenders and twelve evenly spaced controls alike -- cross
$2\times10^{-8}$ at some tolerance on that ladder.  Re-running the identical
computation on a different CPU architecture, same locked
\texttt{numpy}/\texttt{scipy}, reproduces every digit locally but differs from
the frozen values by a median $2.78\times10^{-8}$ and a maximum
$4.14\times10^{-7}$; ten of twenty-two comparable probes land above the gate.
The mechanism is transparent: $\Delta=(\alpha-4)^2-4(\beta-4\alpha+8)$ with
$\alpha,\beta=O(1)$ cancels roughly eight decimal digits, and the surviving
digits sit on architecture-dependent summation order inside the monodromy
product.

Two consequences, stated separately because they are not the same claim.
First, for the roughly $165$ cells in the top decade the accepted float64
residual lies below that lane's own evaluation noise, so $|G|\le
2\times10^{-8}$ there records where the localizer stopped, not how close the
point is to the true critical set.  The same stopping rule is visible in the
independent BigFloat lane, which also terminates on the first iterate under the
gate and consequently reports seven of its $158$ roots at $10^{-8}$ to
$1.66\times10^{-8}$ despite $40$-digit arithmetic.
Second, the \emph{locations} are much better than the certificate.  The measured
$\partial G/\partial m_2$ ranges from $20$ to $660$, so even the full evaluation
uncertainty maps to an $m_2$ uncertainty between $10^{-10}$ and $8\times10^{-9}$,
far inside the $10^{-3}$ published cells.  The graph's combinatorics are not at
risk; the numerical certificate attached to them is weaker than its own
tolerance suggests.

The tail does not cluster.  Among the twenty-five worst cells the event-mode
enrichment is $1.27$ for $\Delta$, $1.00$ for $+1$ and $0.59$ for $-1$;
orientation enrichment is $1.07$ versus $0.93$; they occupy twelve distinct
$m_1$ bands; the Spearman correlation between $|G|$ and $m_1$ is $0.12$; and
their median distance to the nearest organizer, $0.054$, is indistinguishable
from the census median $0.049$.  A physical cause would concentrate.  This is
scattered, which is what a measurement floor looks like.

That floor is quantifiable.  Across the tight tolerance ladder the monodromy
invariants $\alpha,\beta$ themselves are reproducible only to a median relative
$4.2\times10^{-9}$ -- about nine significant digits, not fifteen -- and the
event functionals cancel by a median factor $2.6\times10^{8}$ (worst
$5.2\times10^{10}$).  The product of those two numbers is the observed
$10^{-7}$.  Pulling $\max|G|$ down by a factor of ten to $2\times10^{-9}$ is
therefore not reachable in float64 at any integrator tolerance: the target sits
$73$ times below that lane's own evaluation uncertainty.  It is reachable in the
BigFloat lane, which already attains $7\times10^{-13}$ on its best cells and
whose $10^{-8}$ entries are gate-limited rather than precision-limited; the cost
is running all $620$ cells there instead of $158$.  At the measured mean of
$5.45$ secant iterations per cell and the measured dps$=60$ correction cost of
$5.5$ minutes, that is about $23$ minutes per cell, $179$ serial CPU-hours, or
under three wall-hours at the existing $62$-way CI fan-out.

Accordingly every solve in this work now reports, alongside its residual, the
singular values $\sigma_{\min}$, $\sigma_{\max}$, the condition number
$\kappa_2=\sigma_{\max}/\sigma_{\min}$ of its Jacobian, and the backward-error
displacement bound $\|\delta x\|\lesssim\|J^{+}\|\,\|F(x)\|$.  A residual
without its conditioning is not a measurement.
