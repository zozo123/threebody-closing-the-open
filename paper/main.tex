\begin{abstract}
The Li--Li--Liao 2021 catalog reports $135{,}445$ unequal-mass non-hierarchical
periodic orbits and $13{,}315$ linearly stable samples on a $0.001$ mass grid.
A 2023 analysis of the same table argued that the set is two families.
We treat family identity as continuation connectivity, not as a plot of
scale-invariant invariants, and we treat the linear-stability boundary as the
preimage of a universal Floquet domain rather than as a rectangular S/U mask.

\textbf{Earned results.}
(i)~The frozen catalog is supported as one continuation-connected component in
the declared gauge; every adversarially chosen cut that could have separated it
was crossed bidirectionally.
(ii)~On the slice $m_1\approx0.80234$ the lower transition is an independently
reproduced physical $+1$ crossing, bracketed to $m_2$ width
$1.58\times10^{-9}$.
(iii)~On the slice $m_1\approx0.80229$ the upper transition is an independently
reproduced opposite-Krein Hamiltonian--Hopf event, bracketed to $m_2$ width
$6.27\times10^{-9}$.
(iv)~Near each of the three coarse $+1/-1$ switches there is a root of
$G_+=G_-=0$, recomputed at $60$ digits in an independent stack; those three
artifacts sit at the \emph{high-precision supported} rung of the evidence
ladder, one rung below the two bracketed events.

\textbf{Not earned.}
The connected critical graph is unfinished.
All $620$ published S/U cells are now localized once and assigned to seven
mechanism polylines, but four edge ends remain unclassified, so the assembled
edge incidence is still two components rather than one; the secondary-lobe
birth and death are unclassified; the lower $+1$ daughter is unclassified;
and completeness is not frozen.
Scientific status remains OPEN.
This is not a solution of the general three-body problem, and ``stable'' here
means planar linear Floquet stability only --- not nonlinear stability, not
KAM stability, and not spatial stability.
No priority claim is made.
\end{abstract}

\section{The problem, and what it is not}

The general Newtonian three-body problem is not being solved.
The v1 question is finite \cite{li2021stable,stancevic2023moth,li2025review}:

\begin{quote}
Is the Li--Li--Liao unequal-mass non-hierarchical catalog one
continuation-connected family or several?
On each component, what is the planar linear-Floquet critical graph in the
$(m_1,m_2)$ plane at $m_3=1$?
\end{quote}

Li, Li and Liao continued one family on $m_3=1$, $m_1\in[0.8,1.1]$,
$m_2\in[0.7,1.2]$, spacing $0.001$, and plotted a stable domain
\cite{li2021stable}.
Those $135{,}445$ orbits and $13{,}315$ stable labels are literature facts.
ATLAS may not claim them.

Stan\v{c}evi\'c, Vasiljevi\'c and Dmitra\v{s}inovi\'c argued that the same
table is two independent sets, using mass-corrected period and angular-momentum
relations \cite{stancevic2023moth}.
That is the sharpest competing reading of the exact catalog.
We accept their diagnostic as a plot, and reject it as a definition of a
dynamical family.

A later review confirms that unequal-mass continuation, topology, and
high-precision linear stability remain active \cite{li2025review}.
Equal-mass stable-orbit searches \cite{hristov2025stable} and restricted-problem
continuation papers are methodological neighbors, not this catalog.

Gate~C has passed.

\section{Equations and the universal Floquet domain}

Planar Newtonian motion with $G=1$ is
\begin{equation}
\ddot{\mathbf r}_i=\sum_{j\ne i}m_j\frac{\mathbf r_j-\mathbf r_i}{\lVert\mathbf r_j-\mathbf r_i\rVert^3}.
\end{equation}
Screening uses DOP853 in IEEE binary64.
Publication truth uses an independent Julia BigFloat Vern9 stack and the
physical symplectic quotient $E^\omega/E$ in canonical Jacobi coordinates,
$E=\mathrm{span}\{X_H,X_L\}$, not deletion of four raw multipliers.

After center-of-mass reduction the monodromy has four trivial/symmetry unit
multipliers.
The remaining four form two reciprocal pairs.
With $t=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}$,
\[
P(t)=t^2-(\alpha-4)t+\beta-4\alpha+8,
\]
where $\alpha=\mathrm{tr}\,M$ and
$\beta=((\mathrm{tr}\,M)^2-\mathrm{tr}(M^2))/2$.
Spectral stability of both pairs is equivalent to both roots of $P$ lying in
$[-2,2]$.
The three walls are
\begin{align}
P(+2)&=\beta-6\alpha+20=0,\\
P(-2)&=\beta-2\alpha+4=0,\\
\Delta&=\alpha^2+8\alpha-16-4\beta=0.
\end{align}
The exact spectral vertices are
$(0,-4)$ (double $-1$), $(4,4)$ (mixed $+1/-1$), and $(8,28)$ (double $+1$).
A smooth $+1/-1$ switch on one continuation sheet must pass through $(4,4)$
unless a coarse cell aliases several roots.
The mass-space critical set is the preimage of this universal boundary under
the continuation-sheet map $(m_1,m_2)\mapsto(\alpha,\beta)$.

\section{Screening picture, not a release graph}

On the published $0.001$ grid every one of the $620$ adjacent S/U cells has
exactly one endpoint-sign-changing smooth event:
$198$ of type $+1$, $168$ of type $-1$, $254$ trace collisions.
Coarse tracks:
principal lower $+1\to-1\to+1$,
principal upper $\Delta=0$,
secondary lower $-1\to+1$,
secondary upper $-1$.
That census is a seed, not the graph.
Its worst localized residual is $\lvert\text{event}\rvert=1.9898\times10^{-8}$
against the frozen gate $2\times10^{-8}$, a margin of about $0.5\%$, with $165$
of the $620$ roots above $10^{-8}$; the gate is cleared, but not comfortably.
An off-grid active-learning screen of $12$ proposals all corrected onto the
known sheet and none were screening-stable: no hidden island in that sample.

\paragraph{How little ``completeness'' covers.}
The word \emph{complete} is used here only in a bounded sense, and the bound is
small.  The finest completeness input is the stability-neck raster, frozen over
$m_1\in[0.997,0.999]$, $m_2\in[0.993,1.006]$ at step $10^{-4}$
($2751$ samples).  That rectangle is
$2.6\times10^{-5}$ of the $0.15$ area of the declared domain
$m_1\in[0.8,1.1]$, $m_2\in[0.7,1.2]$: \textbf{0.0173\%}.
Everywhere else, ``no hidden stability pocket'' rests on the published $0.001$
grid and on a $12$-proposal off-grid sample.
The figure is derived, not asserted: \texttt{scripts/completeness\_scope.py}
reads the raster extent from the merge job of
\texttt{.github/workflows/stability-neck-scan.yml} and the declared domain from
\texttt{scripts/assemble\_critical\_graph.py}, and a test fails if the number
quoted here drifts from it.

\paragraph{The assembled graph is not yet connected.}
Continuation-connectedness of the family sheet (Gate~C) and connectedness of
the critical graph are different statements, and the second does not follow from
the first.  In the committed
\texttt{research/evidence/V1\_CRITICAL\_GRAPH.json}, four edge ends are still
unclassified, so the edge incidence splits into two components ---
$\{$domain-$m_1$-min, mixed-L, mixed-R, domain-$m_2$-max$\}$ and
$\{$mixed-S$\}$ --- while five further declared nodes carry no edge at all.
Attachment of an edge end to an organizer is also threshold-dependent: the
assembler binds germs within a mass distance
$\texttt{GERM\_ATTACH\_DISTANCE}=0.008$, and sweeping that constant shows the
present six-attachment assembly survives only for thresholds in
$[0.006351,0.009933]$, a window about $1.56\times$ wide.

\section{What remains, and why the status is OPEN}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Classify the four unclassified edge ends so the graph's edge incidence is
one connected object rather than two components plus five unattached nodes.
\item Classify the secondary-left $G_-$ birth (fold candidate near
$(0.9957,0.9743)$) and the secondary-right death between $m_1=1.042$ and
$1.043$.
\item Attach event arcs through the three mixed roots.
\item Classify the physical-soft $+1$ daughter, or falsify it.
\item Flip \texttt{release\_ready} only with
\texttt{scripts/assemble\_critical\_graph.py}.
\item Rerun the novelty search on the freeze day and run
\texttt{--require-solved}.
\end{enumerate}
Until those close, a sentence of the form ``we computed the connected
stability-boundary manifold'' is false.
