\section{Catalog, normalization, and terminology}

The Li--Li--Liao table contains $135{,}445$ non-hierarchical unequal-mass
planar periodic-orbit samples on
$m_1\in[0.8,1.1]$, $m_2\in[0.7,1.2]$, $m_3=1$, with mass spacing $0.001$.
The source reports $13{,}315$ samples as linearly stable
\cite{li2021stable}. The numerical archive accepts the upstream bytes only
when their Git blob is
\texttt{79b2963df43e62201c35690bfc22bec166132427}.

The scale-invariant coordinates used in the projection analysis are
\begin{equation}
T_{\rm si}=T\lvert E\rvert^{3/2}M^{-5/2},\qquad
L_{\rm si}=L\lvert E\rvert^{1/2}M^{-13/6},
\end{equation}
where $M=m_1+m_2+m_3$. The corresponding map is
$\Psi(m_1,m_2)=(T_{\rm si},L_{\rm si})$.

Every catalog entry carries the same reported syzygy word $bABabaBAba$.
Topological-word conventions follow the standard shape-sphere construction
\cite{montgomery1995geometricphase,moeckel2012regularization}. The present
component classification is determined by continuation of periodic solutions.

\section{Continuation and branch matching}

The published samples use the collinear shooting chart
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf r_1&=(x_1,0),&\quad \mathbf r_2&=(1,0),&\quad \mathbf r_3&=(0,0),\\
\mathbf v_1&=(0,v_1),& \mathbf v_2&=(0,v_2),
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with $\mathbf v_3$ fixed by zero total momentum. At fixed masses, the shooting
unknowns are $(x_1,v_1,v_2,T)$. A neighboring solution supplies the predictor;
a Newton correction in center-of-mass-reduced coordinates restores periodicity.
A connection is accepted when the continuation converges in both directions and
the terminal shooting coordinates match the independently corrected endpoint.

The $135{,}445$ samples form a connected mass-adjacency graph with $270{,}087$
adjacent links and a spanning tree containing $135{,}444$ edges. Twenty-six
connections were selected to maximize indicators of possible separation:
five macroscopic bottlenecks, the twenty largest chart discontinuities along
the spanning tree, and one long-range pair with nearly coincident invariant
images. Bidirectional continuation succeeds for all $26$.

The long-range pair is
\begin{equation}
(m_1,m_2)_A=(1.072,1.066),\qquad
(m_1,m_2)_B=(1.100,1.121),
\end{equation}
with Euclidean mass distance $0.0617$. Eighty continuation steps in each
direction give maximum shooting residual below $2.3\times10^{-10}$ and terminal
normalized chart mismatch below $6\times10^{-13}$. The same endpoints were also
connected in a translation-reduced strict-periodic formulation outside the
published collinear-velocity ansatz.

\section{Invariant-projection audit}

Centered finite differences of $\Psi$ on the published grid produce
$134{,}046$ interior Jacobian estimates. Their normalized determinants have
both signs: $113{,}514$ positive and $20{,}532$ negative. There are $590$
adjacent determinant-sign-change edges, whose incident nodes form one connected
locus in the sampled mass plane. The minimum normalized singular-value ratio is
approximately $1.39\times10^{-6}$.

A nearest-neighbor search in standardized $(T_{\rm si},L_{\rm si})$ coordinates
also identifies distant masses with nearly coincident invariant images.
Among rows whose selected invariant neighbor is at least $0.05$ away in mass,
$86$ pairs have standardized invariant distance below $10^{-4}$. The
long-range continuation pair above is one of these near coincidences. These
statistics describe a discrete singularity and non-unique appearance of the
sampled projection; the continuation test supplies the dynamical connection.

\section{Physical Floquet reduction}

The variational equations are integrated in canonical Jacobi coordinates. The
Hamiltonian and angular-momentum neutral directions span
$E=\operatorname{span}\{X_H,X_L\}$ and are removed through the quotient
$E^{\omega}/E$. The resulting physical return map $M_{\rm ph}$ is a
$4\times4$ symplectic matrix. Define
\begin{equation}
a=\operatorname{tr}M_{\rm ph},\qquad
b=\frac{(\operatorname{tr}M_{\rm ph})^2-
\operatorname{tr}(M_{\rm ph}^2)}{2}.
\end{equation}
Then
\begin{equation}
\chi(\lambda)=\lambda^4-a\lambda^3+b\lambda^2-a\lambda+1
\end{equation}
and, for $t=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}$,
\begin{equation}
P(t)=t^2-at+b-2.
\end{equation}
The physical critical functions are
\begin{equation}
G_+=b-2a+2,\qquad
G_-=b+2a+2,\qquad
\Delta=a^2-4b+8.
\end{equation}
The physical spectral vertices are $(-4,6)$ (double $-1$), $(0,-2)$ (mixed
$+1/-1$), and $(4,6)$ (double $+1$).

Some archived screening records use invariants derived from the unreduced
$8\times8$ monodromy. They are related to the physical invariants by
\begin{equation}
\alpha=a+4,\qquad \beta=b+4a+6.
\end{equation}
Thus the three physical vertices map to $(0,-4)$, $(4,4)$, and $(8,28)$ in the
archive convention. The mechanism labels and mass coordinates are unchanged by
this affine change of coordinates.

\section{High-precision $+1$ transition}

At $m_1=0.8023446113666945$, an independent $60$-digit implementation encloses
the lower transition in
\begin{equation}
0.7540187211922449\le m_2\le0.7540187227698909,
\end{equation}
with bracket width $1.577646\times10^{-9}$ and a sign change of $G_+$. On the
smaller-$m_2$ flank the relevant physical multipliers form a real reciprocal
pair approximately $(0.9748,1.0258)$. On the larger-$m_2$ flank the pair is
approximately $0.99968\pm0.02547i$ on the unit circle. The reduced closures are
of order $10^{-21}$ and the physical symplectic defects are of order $10^{-19}$.

\section{High-precision Hamiltonian--Hopf transition}

At $m_1=0.8022889780964406$, the upper transition lies in
\begin{equation}
0.7592347034247101\le m_2\le0.7592347096924873,
\end{equation}
with bracket width $6.267777\times10^{-9}$ and a sign change of $\Delta$. On
the stable flank the upper-half unit modes are
$0.40876+0.91264i$ and $0.44163+0.89720i$. Their Krein forms have opposite

deviation $1.83\times10^{-2}$. The reduced closures are of order $10^{-22}$ and
the physical symplectic defects are of order $10^{-20}$.

\section{Broader critical survey and numerical sensitivity}

The published table contains $620$ adjacent cells whose reported linear
stability label changes. The survey localizes one critical point in each cell:
$198$ of type $G_+$, $168$ of type $G_-$, and $254$ of type $\Delta$. A
full-domain sign sweep contributes additional sampled event curves. This survey
is retained as a map of sampled event preimages; it is not used as an exhaustive
classification of every critical curve.

Of the $620$ published transition cells, $462$ retain binary64 localization and
$158$ have arbitrary-precision corroboration. Equivalent double-precision
coordinate formulations move the event value by more than $2\times10^{-8}$ on
$345$ of the $620$ cells. Arbitrary-precision escalation of sixteen difficult
cells changed four mechanism labels from $G_+$ to $\Delta$. These sensitivity
results are the reason the two headline transitions are reported from the
independent $60$-digit calculation.

Two sampled survey ends remain unclassified, near $(0.892,0.75308)$ and
$(1.042,0.85798)$. They do not affect the continuation-component statement or
the two representative high-precision transitions reported in the Letter.
