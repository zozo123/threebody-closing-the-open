\section{Catalog, normalization, and notation}

The upstream table of Li, Li, and Liao contains $135{,}445$ unequal-mass
non-hierarchical planar periodic orbits on the mass grid
$m_1\in[0.8,1.1]$, $m_2\in[0.7,1.2]$ at $m_3=1$ with spacing $0.001$, of which
$13{,}315$ samples are reported as linearly stable~\cite{li2021stable}.
The archive used here accepts that table only at Git blob
\texttt{79b2963df43e62201c35690bfc22bec166132427}~\cite{eliaz2026archive}.
Newtonian gravity is taken with $G=1$.
Scale-invariant coordinates are
\begin{equation}
T_{\rm si}=T\,\lvert E\rvert^{3/2}M^{-5/2},\qquad
L_{\rm si}=L\,\lvert E\rvert^{1/2}M^{-13/6},
\end{equation}
with $M=m_1+m_2+m_3$, matching the mass-corrected representation analyzed by
Stan{\v c}evi{\'c} \emph{et al.}~\cite{stancevic2023moth}.
The maps used in the Letter are
$\Psi(m_1,m_2)=(T_{\rm si},L_{\rm si})$ and
$\Phi(m_1,m_2)=(\alpha,\beta)$.

\section{Topological-word conventions}

Every catalog member is published with the syzygy word $bABabaBAba$.
Removing the three binary-collision points from the shape sphere leaves a
space whose fundamental group is free on two generators, so a collisionless
periodic orbit determines a conjugacy class in $F_2$: a cyclic word modulo
free reduction and rotation, with an uppercase letter denoting the inverse
generator~\cite{montgomery1995geometricphase,moeckel2012regularization}.
The published word is already freely and cyclically reduced and rotates to
$ABabaBAbab$.
Letter count and collinear-crossing count are distinct quantities; the number
of syzygies per period is not re-derived here.
The word is catalog metadata.
Component identity in this work is determined by continuation of periodic
solutions~\cite{chenciner2000figureeight,li2025review}.

\section{Continuation algorithm and branch matching}

Published samples occupy the collinear shooting chart
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf r_1&=(x_1,0),& \mathbf r_2&=(1,0),& \mathbf r_3&=(0,0),\\
\mathbf v_1&=(0,v_1),& \mathbf v_2&=(0,v_2).
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
with $\mathbf v_3$ fixed by vanishing total momentum.
The shooting unknowns at fixed masses are $(x_1,v_1,v_2,T)$.
The corrector is applied in eight-dimensional center-of-mass-reduced
coordinates, which remove the translational kernel before the Newton step.
A neighboring catalog point supplies the predictor.
A connection is recorded when bidirectional continuation converges and the
terminal shooting charts match.

\section{Mass-adjacency graph and spanning tree}

The $135{,}445$ sampled solutions form a connected mass-adjacency graph with
$270{,}087$ links of grid step $0.001$ and a spanning tree of $135{,}444$
edges.
Connectivity of that graph is the starting combinatorial fact; dynamical
connectivity is the continuation of periodic solutions along selected links.

\section{Selection and results of the 26 difficult connections}

Twenty-six links were selected as the strongest available tests of branch
separation:
\begin{enumerate}
\item five macroscopic bottlenecks of the spanning tree, including a cut
separating about $40\%$ of the tree, with terminal normalized chart mismatch
below $10^{-12}$ in both directions;
\item the twenty globally largest chart discontinuities along the spanning
tree, continued with $6$, $12$, $24$, and $48$ substeps as required, all
passing;
\item one long-range pair at $(m_1,m_2)=(1.072,1.066)$ and $(1.100,1.121)$,
mass distance $0.061717$, chosen because the invariant images nearly coincide.
\end{enumerate}
Bidirectional continuation succeeded on all $26$ links.
The rank-$5$ spanning-tree edge at $m_2=0.705$ required $24$ substeps;
endpoint corrected residuals were $1.20\times10^{-9}$ and $1.13\times10^{-9}$.
A $647$-point shooting-rank recensus, after re-correction of the $28$
lowest-rank samples, left a minimum scaled rank ratio $1.08\times10^{-5}$ and
maximum closure $1.80\times10^{-8}$.

\section{Generic strict-periodic cross-check}

The long-range pair was repeated in a translation-reduced strict-periodic
formulation that does not impose the collinear-velocity ansatz.
Both directions passed, with closure of order $10^{-9}$ and terminal matches
of order $10^{-9}$.
The remaining $25$ links were continued in the published collinear chart.

\section{Invariant-projection audit}

Centered finite differences of $\Psi$ on the published grid produce $134{,}046$
interior Jacobian estimates.
The normalized determinant takes both signs: $113{,}514$ positive and
$20{,}532$ negative.
There are $590$ adjacent determinant-sign-change edges, and those nodes form
one connected locus in the sampled mass plane.
The smallest normalized singular-value ratio is about $1.39\times10^{-6}$.
A neighbor search in the standardized $(T_{\rm si},L_{\rm si})$ plane finds
$27{,}111$ rows whose nearest invariant neighbor is at least $0.05$ away in
mass; $86$ of those far-mass pairs have standardized invariant distance below
$10^{-4}$.
The long-range pair of Sec.~V is one such near coincidence.
These counts identify a singular locus of $\Psi$ and a many-to-one character
of the sampled projection; they are a discrete audit of the published grid.

\section{Floquet reduction and spectral invariants}

The publication monodromy is computed in canonical Jacobi coordinates and
reduced to the physical quotient $E^{\omega}/E$,
$E=\mathrm{span}\{X_H,X_L\}$, yielding a $4\times 4$ symplectic matrix
$M$~\cite{yakubovich1975,howard1987linear,frauenfelder2023symplectic,galan2002figureeight}.
With $\alpha=\mathrm{tr}\,M$ and
$\beta=\bigl((\mathrm{tr}\,M)^2-\mathrm{tr}(M^2)\bigr)/2$,
\begin{equation}
P(t)=t^2-(\alpha-4)t+\beta-4\alpha+8,\qquad t=\lambda+\lambda^{-1}.
\end{equation}
Spectral stability of both reciprocal pairs is equivalent to both roots of
$P$ lying in $[-2,2]$.
The walls are $G_+=P(+2)$, $G_-=P(-2)$, and the discriminant $\Delta$ of $P$.
The exact spectral vertices are $(0,-4)$ (double $-1$), $(4,4)$ (mixed
$+1/-1$), and $(8,28)$ (double $+1$).

\section{High-precision $(+1)$ transition}

On the slice $m_1=0.8023446113666945$, $m_3=1$, an independent $60$-digit
Verner integrator with the physical quotient encloses the lower transition by
the $m_2$ bracket
\begin{equation}
[0.7540187211922449,\,0.7540187227698909]
\end{equation}
of width $1.577646\times10^{-9}$, with a sign change of the $+1$ event
function.
The smaller-$m_2$ flank carries a real reciprocal pair
$(\lambda,1/\lambda)\approx(0.9748,1.0258)$ and one elliptic pair.
The larger-$m_2$ flank places the formerly real pair on the unit circle near
$+1$ at $0.99968\pm 0.02547\,i$.
Reduced closures are of order $10^{-21}$; physical symplectic defects are of
order $10^{-19}$.

\section{High-precision Hamiltonian--Hopf transition}

On the slice $m_1=0.8022889780964406$, the upper transition is enclosed by
\begin{equation}
m_2\in[0.7592347034247101,\,0.7592347096924873]
\end{equation}
of width $6.267777\times10^{-9}$, with a sign change of $\Delta$.
On the stable flank the two upper-half unit modes are
$0.40876+0.91264\,i$ and $0.44163+0.89720\,i$, with Krein forms of opposite
sign $+3.76\times10^{-3}$ and $-3.72\times10^{-3}$.
On the unstable flank the spectrum is a reciprocal complex quartet with
radius deviation $1.83\times10^{-2}$ and no remaining upper-half unit modes.
Reduced closures are of order $10^{-22}$; physical symplectic defects are of
order $10^{-20}$.

\section{Full-domain critical survey}

The published table contains $620$ adjacent cells whose linear-stability
label changes.
Each cell was localized once and assigned to a mechanism polyline:
$198$ of type $G_+$, $168$ of type $G_-$, and $254$ of type $\Delta$.
Six additional event-sign polylines from a full-domain sweep contribute $153$
samples, giving a thirteen-edge assembly in mass space
(Fig.~\ref{fig:graph}).
Of the $620$ cells, $462$ rest on binary64 localization and $158$ on
arbitrary-precision corroboration.
The assembly is a survey of sampled event preimages, not a complete topology
of every critical curve.

\input{figS1_critical_graph}

Two finite-lattice ends of that survey remain unclassified, at
$(0.892,0.75308)$ and $(1.042,0.85798)$.
Orbits still close at those ends, with re-corrected closures
$6.6\times10^{-8}$ and $3.9\times10^{-8}$.
Continuing outward renders the event function non-finite after travel of
$1.4\times10^{-3}$ and $2.2\times10^{-3}$ in $m_2$.
An existence-boundary classification was implemented with fail-closed
criteria (divergence rather than a marginal miss, invariance under step
refinement and two higher-precision paths, independent audit, and outward
travel); on the present record it is awarded to neither end.

\section{Numerical sensitivity of the full-domain survey}

Across $775$ localized roots the largest absolute event value is
$1.9898\times10^{-8}$.
Re-deriving the monodromy through a coordinate rotation at identical
tolerance moves the event by more than $2\times10^{-8}$ on $345$ of the $620$
published cells and by more than $2\times10^{-7}$ on $144$ cells, concentrated
in $G_+$ ($126/198$) and $\Delta$ ($217/254$), with $G_-$ ($2/168$) largely
unaffected.
On a seeded sample of $160$ roots, closures written from the screening
integration (relative tolerance $2\times10^{-10}$) are optimistic relative to
the event evaluation (relative tolerance $5\times10^{-13}$) by a median factor
$48.6$.
Arbitrary-precision escalation of sixteen binary64-failed cells returned four
as $\Delta$ collisions where binary64 had recorded $G_+$.
These observations motivate reporting the headline transitions from the
independent $60$-digit calculation.

A $C^1$ variational interval integrator was attempted at five seeds.
Four attempts return no finite enclosure.
The remaining enclosure excludes collision but bounds the Floquet invariants
only at scales $\alpha\sim10^{12}$ and $\beta\sim10^{24}$, with a discriminant
interval that includes both signs and a closure interval of width
$3.1\times10^{-3}$.
That record is retained as a negative validation experiment; it is not used
as an existence proof.

\section{Search and coverage diagnostics}

A frozen raster over
$m_1\in[0.997,0.999]\times m_2\in[0.993,1.012]$ at step $10^{-4}$ comprises
$4011$ samples ($0.0253\%$ of the declared rectangle).
An additional twelve-point off-grid screen occupies $0.002539\%$ of the
rectangle.
Even-order root pairs and tangencies are outside the reach of a sign-change
survey at these resolutions.
Sign-vector audits that require converged coverage of the full declared
$m_2$ interval encounter a large non-existence region at small $m_2$, where
periodic closure of this family fails with residuals many orders above
$10^{-7}$.
Those diagnostics bound the survey; they are not used as Letter claims.
