# Announcement draft

New preprint: direct continuation connects the two branches previously seen in the scale-invariant period-angular-momentum representation of the 135,445-orbit Li-Li-Liao unequal-mass three-body catalog. The same continuation component contains a +1 Floquet transition and an opposite-Krein Hamiltonian-Hopf transition.

The paper, source, and numerical records are available at: [arXiv link] / https://github.com/zozo123/threebody-closing-the-open
