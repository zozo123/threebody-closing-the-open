Periodic-orbit families are routinely classified through low-dimensional invariant diagrams, although projected branches need not preserve the connectivity of the underlying solution manifold. We resolve this ambiguity in a 135,445-orbit unequal-mass three-body catalog by direct numerical continuation, connecting two previously separated invariant branches within one sampled continuation component. High-precision Floquet analysis further identifies distinct +1 and opposite-Krein Hamiltonian-Hopf stability mechanisms along the same component. The distinction between projected branches and continuation components, together with the explicit symplectic mechanisms, is relevant to the geometric classification and stability analysis of parameter-dependent periodic-orbit families throughout Hamiltonian dynamics.
