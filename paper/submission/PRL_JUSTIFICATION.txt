Periodic-orbit families are commonly classified through low-dimensional invariant diagrams, although projected branches need not preserve the connectivity of the underlying solution manifold. We resolve this ambiguity in a 135,445-orbit unequal-mass three-body catalog by direct numerical continuation, connecting two previously separated invariant branches within one sampled continuation component. High-precision Floquet analysis further identifies distinct +1 and opposite-Krein Hamiltonian-Hopf stability mechanisms along the same component. The result provides a general lesson for the geometric classification and stability analysis of parameter-dependent periodic-orbit families in Hamiltonian dynamics, making the work relevant beyond this specific three-body catalog.
